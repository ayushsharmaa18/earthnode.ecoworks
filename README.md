# Earthnode Ecoworks - Official Website

A premium, inspiring website for Earthnode Ecoworks - an environmental movement based in India focused on creating measurable environmental impact through community action, technology, and collaboration.

## 🌱 Vision
To create a world where communities, innovation and nature thrive together through measurable environmental actions.

## 🎯 Mission
To empower individuals and organizations to contribute toward environmental conservation through collaboration, technology, education, and community-driven initiatives.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy
- **Premium & Inspiring**: Feels like Apple, Patagonia, WWF, Stripe, Linear, Notion
- **Minimal**: Lots of whitespace, large typography, beautiful imagery
- **Emotional Impact**: Creates trust, hope, purpose, and professionalism within 5 seconds
- **Thoughtful Animations**: Fade, parallax, scroll reveal, floating leaves, soft gradients
- **Pixel Perfect**: Responsive on desktop, tablet, mobile (especially iPhone)

## 🎨 Color Palette
- **Forest Green**: `#042b1d`, `#0d5a3b` (Primary)
- **Emerald Green**: `#10b981`, `#059669` (Secondary/Accent)
- **White**: `#ffffff` (Background/Text)

## 🔤 Typography
- **Headings**: Space Grotesk (Bold, impactful)
- **Body**: Inter (Clean, readable)
- **Accents**: Manrope (Buttons, UI elements)
