Our Story

Built by people who couldn't look away

Earthnode Ecoworks began in 2026 as a small group of volunteers cleaning up a single neighborhood park. Today, we work across 18 states — but the instinct is the same: show up, do the work, bring people along.

Why We Exist

The land, the mind, and the livelihood are one problem

You can't ask a family to protect a forest when their children are hungry. You can't expect a community to thrive on a poisoned river. So we don't pick one fight — we work all three fronts together, in the same villages, with the same people.

Our Approach

How we actually work

01

Community-Led, Not Community-Served

Every project is co-designed with the local community. We bring resources and coordination; they bring the knowledge of what actually works here.

02

Measured, Not Assumed

We track survival rates of saplings, attendance at counselling sessions, and placement rates after training — because good intentions aren't good enough.

03

Built to Outlast Us

Every program has a local handover plan from day one. Our goal in any village is to eventually become unnecessary there.
